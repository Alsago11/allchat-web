AllChat Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026
This is an English translation for your convenience. If it ever differs from the Spanish version, the Spanish version prevails. Read it in Spanish

The key points, in six lines

  • What AllChat learns from you, your dictionary and your settings live in files on your PC and never leave it.
  • Correction is done by an engine installed on your computer. If that engine doesn't start, AllChat would rather not correct than let your text leave your computer: it only leaves if you turn on correction over the internet in Settings, and it comes turned off.
  • Translation does use the internet: the text you translate goes to our server, which sends it to Google Cloud Translation to be translated. The server doesn't store it: it only records how many translations you make, never which ones.
  • From your account we keep your email and your progress. We never keep your sentences, only how many there were.
  • If you pay for Premium, Stripe handles the payment: only Stripe sees your card. We know which email paid, which plan and until when, and nothing else.
  • We don't sell your data to anyone, there are no ads and there's no profiling.

Who the data controller is

The controller of your data is Álvaro Sánchez Gómez, an individual (natural person) domiciled in Spain, creator and owner of AllChat.

For anything related to your data, write to hola@allchat.es. You'll get an answer from us, not from a form.

What NEVER leaves your computer

This is AllChat's central promise and it's written into the code, not just here:

  • What AllChat learns from the way you write. It lives in a file on your PC. You can see how many word pairs it has learned and erase it entirely from Settings, whenever you want.
  • Your personal dictionary and the words you teach it.
  • Your settings: colors, shortcuts, modes, language and who you choose to keep you company.
  • The app log (registro.log). It stays on your PC. It only leaves if you decide to attach it to report a bug, and since version 0.49 that file no longer stores what you type: it records how many words there were, not which ones, and your email appears redacted.

About correction and the local engine. AllChat installs a correction engine on your computer that works without internet, and it's the one that's always used. If that engine can't start on your device, AllChat doesn't correct and tells you so: your text stays on your computer. In Settings, under Modes, there's a switch to allow correction over the internet in that case; it comes turned off and only you turn it on. If you turn it on, the text you correct will travel to LanguageTool's public API for as long as the engine on your PC still fails to start.

What we never do with your text: store it on our server, use it to train anything, sell it or hand it over to anyone for advertising. Our server never stores a sentence of yours: the one you ask to translate passes through it on its way to Google and isn't kept.

Who data is shared with

Only with what's essential for the app to work. None of these services receives data for advertising:

  • Google Cloud Translation (Google): receives the text you translate through our server, to translate it. It's Google's translation service under contract, not the free translator on the web.
  • LanguageTool: receives the text you correct only if you've turned on correction over the internet and the engine on your PC doesn't start. With the switch off, which is how it comes, it receives nothing.
  • Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe, Ireland): charges the Premium subscription and acts as the seller of that purchase, so it processes your email, your country, your card and your invoices as a controller in its own right, under its own privacy policy. We don't receive or store your card number. Stripe notifies us of your sign-ups, renewals and cancellations so that Premium turns on and off in your account.
  • Discord: only if you connect your Discord from Settings. Discord tells us your ID and your username, and our bot adds or removes your role on the AllChat Discord server. We don't send Discord your email or anything from your account.
  • Resend: sends the email with your sign-in link, and needs your address to do so.
  • Hetzner: it's where our server lives, in Germany (European Union).
  • Cloudflare: serves this website and sees the IP address of whoever visits it.
  • GitHub: hosts the channel from which AllChat downloads its updates, so it sees your device's IP when it checks for a new version.

Legal basis

We process your data because it's necessary to provide the service you've asked us for (Article 6(1)(b) of the GDPR): without your email there's no account, and without an account there's no progress to save or premium to recognize. Your Premium subscription data is also necessary for the contract (6(1)(b)): without it we wouldn't know whom to turn Premium on for or whom to refund a charge to. We process the IP address of sign-in attempts on the basis of our legitimate interest in protecting the service from abuse (Article 6(1)(f)).

Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection at any time. Write to hola@allchat.es from your account's email and we'll sort it out.

Deleting your account deletes your email, your progress and your sessions from our server. What AllChat learned from you doesn't need to be deleted by us: we never had it, it's on your PC and you erase it yourself from Settings.

If you think we haven't done things right, you can file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, AEPD), which is Spain's data protection authority.

Minors

AllChat is not aimed at minors under the age of 14, and we don't knowingly collect data from people of that age.

Changes to this policy

If we change something important, we'll change the date above and tell you about it in the app's What's new. AllChat is in beta and growing fast: when a new feature touches data, this page is updated before the feature reaches your computer.
