HOW IT WORKS

Seven things it does while you type.

01 · STAR MODE

Type. Breathe. Already translated.

Type your sentence however it comes out, then pause for a second and a half. AllChat shows it to you corrected and translated in a floating card. Like it? Hit Enter and it's sent. Don't? Keep typing and it goes away.

04 · WHILE YOU TYPE

You see the word before you finish it.

From the second letter, AllChat shows you the words that fit, right where you're typing. Pick yours with Tab and keep going: you type faster and every word comes out spelled right without you having to think about it.

And if typing is hard for you, whether you're slow, type with one finger or mix up your letters, AllChat finishes the word for you. At home, even the folks who “aren't computer people” will love it.

Learning a language? Set it as “Your language” and AllChat feeds you the word you can't think of, while you actually chat.

05 · IT BECOMES YOURS

The more you type, the better it gets you.

AllChat learns from your sentences: your names, your go-to phrases, the way you talk. Every day it predicts them sooner. And it all stays on your PC: what it learns lives in a little file on your computer, never on a server.

It has its own switch in Settings and an erase button to start from scratch whenever you want. You're in charge.

09 · ABOUT US

Behind AllChat there's one person.

AllChat isn't made by a company or a team. It's made by one person, in Spain, in their spare time and with a whole lot of stubbornness.

It started with something really silly and really real: you play, you talk, you type in a hurry, and half the conversation never makes it out. Because the sentence won't come out in the other person's language, or because you typed it in a rush and you'd rather delete it than send it.

The idea wasn't to make yet another app you have to copy and paste into, but to put the help inside the one you already have open: your Discord, your game, your browser. That's why AllChat floats on top of everything and never asks you to switch windows.

If something doesn't make sense or you have a better idea, get in touch. It's read by the same person who codes it, and that's how half the things you just saw came about.

🔒 Your text doesn't leave your PC

Correction runs on a local engine installed on your computer: what you type doesn't travel to any server to get corrected. If that engine fails, AllChat would rather not correct than send your text off your PC, and only you can change that in Settings. And what AllChat learns from you stays put too: it lives in a file on your PC that you can delete whenever you want. Only translation uses the internet, like any translator.

10 · FAQ

What people ask us, with no fine print.

If it's not here, email us at hola@allchat.es: a real person answers.

Does it work in the Discord app or only on the web?
Both. AllChat doesn't live inside Discord: it works through the Windows keyboard, so you can use it in the Discord desktop app, in Discord in your browser and almost anywhere you type on your PC. There's no version for phones or Mac, though: AllChat is Windows only.
Does it help if I'm learning a language?
It helps you practice while you chat; it doesn't give you lessons. Pick that language as “Your language” (Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian or English) and the mode you want: Prediction, from the second letter, feeds you the word you're looking for and how it's spelled, or Correction shows you your sentence written correctly before you send it, free and without your text leaving your PC. It doesn't explain grammar or pronunciation.
Why does it ask for my email?
To save your level, your progress and, if you have it, your Premium, so you don't lose them if you switch computers. There's no password: you get a link, confirm it and you're in, just once on each computer. We don't send you ads.
Which games won't it work in?
The ones that run as administrator: Windows doesn't let a regular program see what you type in them. AllChat tells you when that happens and, if there's a Windows checkbox that fixes it, takes you to it. It also doesn't help in games without a text chat. We don't have a list of "compatible" games: we only say where we've tested it and it works.
What about anti-cheats?
Some anti-cheats watch for programs that read the keyboard, and AllChat is one of those programs. We don't know if one will ever flag it, and the risk of a ban is yours (it's point 8 of our terms). Some games' rules ban programs that type or act for you: if a game bans it, or you're not sure, don't use it there.
Does it translate voice?
No. AllChat translates what you type and what you read on screen (an area you point at or text you select), but it doesn't listen to your mic or to voice chat.
What's free?
Correcting and predicting, unlimited and forever. Translating, 25 messages a day: one message counts as one even if AllChat translates it several times while you type it. Premium removes that limit for €5.99 a month or €54.99 a year, and you can cancel anytime.
Does my text leave my PC?
To correct it, no: the spell checker is inside AllChat and works offline. To translate it, yes: what you ask to translate goes to our server and from there to Google Cloud Translation, gets translated and isn't stored. What AllChat learns about the way you write stays on your computer. All the details are in the privacy policy.
How much space does it take?
The download is about 390 MB, because it includes the spell checker so it works offline, and once installed it takes up to about 760 MB. It installs for free from the Microsoft Store and needs Windows 10 (version 2004 or later) or 11, 64-bit.
