The important bits, in seven lines

  • AllChat helps you write, but you're the one who sends your messages. Always check what's about to go out.
  • Correction and translation are automatic: they make mistakes. Don't use them for texts where a mistake would have serious consequences.
  • In online games, use it at your own risk. Some anti-cheats don't allow programs that read the keyboard.
  • We're in beta: it may fail, change or stop.
  • What you write is yours. We don't look at it or claim it.
  • Correcting is free forever. Premium renews automatically and you can cancel it whenever you want: if you cancel, you keep it until the end of what you've already paid for.
  • If you're a consumer, your legal rights take precedence over this text, and nothing written here takes them away from you.

3. What AllChat is and what it isn't

AllChat is a writing aid: it corrects, predicts and translates while you type in other applications. It works by suggesting changes that you apply.

AllChat is not:

  • A professional or sworn translation service.
  • A guarantee that your text is correct, appropriate or polite.
  • A spell checker fit for legal, medical or official academic documents, contracts or any text where a changed word has consequences.

4. Who can use it

You need to be at least 14 years old. If you're under 18 and one day sign up for the paid version, you need permission from your parent or guardian.

AllChat reads what you type on your computer so it can correct it. So: only install it on computers that are yours, or on which you have permission to do so. Installing it on someone else's computer without them knowing may be illegal, and that would be on you, not on us.

We give you a personal license to use AllChat on your computers. The program is still ours: you may not resell, rent or distribute it, or try to extract its source code, beyond what the law expressly allows you to do.

Correction can get it wrong

The spell checker is automatic. Sometimes it will swap one word for another, miss a joke, lose a nuance or correct a proper name that was fine. Before you send a message, read it. You're responsible for what you send, just as if you had written it without help.

Translation goes through our server and a third party

Correction works inside your computer, but translating needs internet: the text you translate travels to our server and from there to Google Cloud Translation, which is what translates it. Our server doesn't store what you translate: it only counts how many translations you make, because the free plan has a daily cap. We don't control Google's service and we're not liable for its quality, or for its availability. It's explained in detail in the privacy policy.

Your antivirus may be suspicious

To correct while you type, AllChat needs to read the keyboard. That's also what some malicious programs do, so an antivirus may warn you about it or block it. We can't prevent that and we're not liable for what your antivirus decides.

Using it while you play is your decision and your risk. We don't guarantee that it works in any particular game, and we're not liable for penalties, suspensions or account closures imposed by the game's company. If you have doubts about a game, don't use it there.

11. Premium subscription

AllChat has a free plan, which is free forever, and a paid subscription called Premium. This section explains how the subscription works, with no fine print.

What it includes and what it doesn't

  • With the free plan you get correction, prediction, what AllChat learns from you and a limited number of translations a day. You can see that number on the website and inside the application, in Settings, and it may go up without notice and go down only with notice.
  • With Premium you translate without that daily limit, through any of the application's routes, and you unlock what the website advertises as exclusive to Premium. There is a technical daily cap, far above what a person types, which only serves to stop automated use (point 10).
  • Premium is tied to your account, not to a computer: it works on up to three of your computers at once. If you sign in on a fourth, Premium stays on the three oldest until you sign out on one of them from Settings.

Price and who charges you

  • Premium costs €5.99 a month or €54.99 a year. Prices include VAT: the price you see on the payment page is the one you're charged, with no surprises at the end. If you pay from outside Spain, the payment page may show you the price in your currency and with your country's tax, always before you confirm.
  • Payment is handled by Stripe through Link, its payment service, which acts as the official seller for this purchase: on the payment page and on the receipt you'll see that the purchase is “Sold through Link”. It's Stripe that charges you, sends you the receipt and the invoice from Link, applies your country's taxes and handles refunds and payment disputes. On your statement you'll see the charge as LINK.COM* ALLCHAT.ES. We don't see or store your card: we only know which email paid and until when.
  • You pay on Stripe's payment page, which you reach from allchat.es or from the application. If you pay with an email different from the one on your AllChat account, when the payment is done we give you a redemption key that you paste in Settings > Premium, and the subscription moves to your account.
  • If you're under 18, you need permission from your parent or guardian to sign up for Premium (point 4).

It renews automatically, and this is what that means

Premium is a subscription with automatic renewal. The monthly plan is charged every month and the yearly plan is charged in full in advance and charged again every year, on the same date you signed up, until you cancel it. There's no minimum term: canceling is free and has no penalty.

  • If a renewal charge fails (expired card, insufficient funds), Stripe retries it for a few days and lets you know by email. If it still doesn't go through, Premium switches off and your account stays on the free plan, without losing any of your stuff.
  • If we ever change the price, we'll tell you by email and inside the application at least 30 days before it affects you, and you'll be able to cancel before you're charged the new price. We will never charge you a price different from the one you accepted without that notice.

Cancel whenever you want

  • You can cancel at any time, without giving reasons, from two places: from the application, in Settings > Premium, with the «Manage my subscription» button, which opens the Stripe customer portal (that's also where you change your card and download your invoices); or at link.com, signing in with the email you paid with, where you can also see your purchases.
  • If something isn't working for you, write to us at hola@allchat.es and we'll cancel it for you. A person answers.
  • Canceling doesn't cut off Premium on the spot: you keep it until the end of the period you've already paid for (the month or the year) and from then on you're not charged any more. If you canceled the yearly plan halfway through the year, the remaining part isn't refunded, except for what the next point says.
  • If you change your mind, you can reactivate it from the portal in the application before the paid period ends.

Right of withdrawal and refunds

  • If you're a consumer, you have by law 14 days from your first purchase to withdraw. Premium starts working the moment you pay, with your consent, and even so we make it simple: if you write to us within those 14 days from your first purchase, we refund the full amount, no questions asked. The refund is made by Stripe to the same card, and it usually takes a few days to show up.
  • Renewals don't open a new withdrawal period, because they're the continuation of the same subscription. To avoid paying for the next one, just cancel before the renewal date.
  • If Premium stops working through our fault for more than a few days, or if we remove something we advertised as included, we refund you the proportional part of the affected time, or we make it up to you with more Premium time if you prefer.

If one day AllChat shuts down or stops offering Premium, we'll give advance notice, stop charging and refund the proportional part of what you've already paid that you have yet to enjoy.
